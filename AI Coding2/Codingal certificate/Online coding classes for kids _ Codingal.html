Guess My Favorite Things

PetPal

Design a conversational bot that engages users in a fun guessing game about their preferences for sweet or savory foods and bright or dark colors.

11:59 PM KST, Sep 02, 2025

Due date of submission

Goal

 

Step 1: Define Intents

  1. Start Conversation Intent
    • Engage the user with a greeting and explain the game.
  2. Animal-Type Intents
    • Small Furry Animal Intent
    • Aquatic Animal Intent
  3. Specific Animal Guessing Intents
    • Rabbit Guess Intent
    • Hamster Guess Intent
    • Goldfish Guess Intent
    • Turtle Guess Intent

 

Step 2: Write Utterances and Responses

Each intent must include multiple utterances and a single response. See examples below.

Intents, Utterances, and Responses

Start Conversation Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "Hi, can we play?"
    • "Guess my pet!"
    • "Hi!"
    • "Hello!"
  • Response:
    "Hi! I’m PetPal, your pet buddy. Let me guess your favorite pet! Do you prefer furry or aquatic animals?"

 

Small Furry Animal Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "I like small furry animals."
    • "It’s a cuddly pet."
    • "Furry and cute, for sure."
  • Response:
    "Cool! Is your pet playful like a rabbit or cozy like a hamster?"

 

Rabbit Guess Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "I want a pet that hops."
    • "It’s playful and active."
    • "Hopping and cuddly."
    • "It has long ears."
  • Response:
    "My guess is that your favorite pet is a rabbit! ???? Rabbits are playful, love to hop around and make adorable companions. Am I right?"

 

Hamster Guess Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "I want a pet that’s cozy."
    • "It’s small and likes to hide."
    • "A pet that runs in a wheel."
    • "Tiny and furry."
  • Response:
    "I think your favorite pet is a hamster! ???? Hamsters are small, curious, and love running on wheels. Did I get it right?"

 

Aquatic Animal Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "I like aquatic animals."
    • "It’s a water pet."
    • "I prefer animals that live in water."
    • "A pet that swims."
  • Response:
    "Got it! Do you want a pet that swims gracefully like a goldfish or one that’s calm and steady like a turtle?"

 

Goldfish Guess Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "I want a pet that swims."
    • "It’s small and colorful."
    • "A pet that lives in a bowl."
    • "Graceful and shiny."
  • Response:
    "It must be a goldfish! ???? Goldfish are colorful, elegant swimmers and super easy to take care of. Did I guess it right?"

 

Turtle Guess Intent:

  • Utterances:
    • "I want a pet that’s calm."
    • "It’s slow and steady."
    • "A pet with a shell."
    • "Quiet and peaceful."
  • Response:
    "I think your favorite pet is a turtle! ???? Turtles are calm, long-living, and make fascinating companions. Am I right?"

 

Step 3: Test the Chatbot

Simulate different conversations to test if the chatbot can navigate through intents correctly and guess the user’s favorite pet based on their inputs.

 

 

Story

 

PetPal is your friendly chatbot buddy who loves learning about your favorite pets! Through a fun series of questions, PetPal narrows down your preferences to guess if your favorite pet is a rabbit, hamster, goldfish, or turtle. This project shows how conversational AI can create engaging and interactive experiences.
